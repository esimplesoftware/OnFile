Onfile Help · License

License

Onfile needs a license key. Until the key is activated, only File → Exit and the Help menu work.

Activate

  1. Open Help → License….
  2. Paste the key from your receipt email. Onfile keys start with ONFILE-.
  3. Click Activate. The badge turns green and shows ✓ Active.

Search, volumes, the hotkey, scans, and live update start as soon as the key is active. You do not need to restart Onfile.

Two PCs per key

One key works on two PCs. To move Onfile to another PC, open Help → License on the old PC and click Deactivate. That frees the slot. A third PC shows “already used on the allowed number of PCs” until one is deactivated.

Offline use

Onfile confirms the key each time it starts. If the PC is offline, Onfile keeps working for 30 days after Last confirmed. After that it locks until it can reach the license server again.

Deactivate

Deactivate frees this PC’s slot and locks Onfile. Your volumes and index stay on disk and come back when you activate again. Deactivate needs an internet connection.

Where the key is stored

license.dat in the Onfile data folder, encrypted for this Windows user. A copy on another PC or user account cannot be read.
